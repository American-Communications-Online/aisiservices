## Language and textual studies

The project uses **wordology** for comparison of terms, translations, and cultural contexts. This is a project term, distinct from the established disciplines of etymology (word histories) and epistemology (the basis of knowledge). Sumerian, Hebrew, Greek, Latin and Coptic have distinct linguistic histories. The **Monad** has different meanings across philosophical and religious traditions, which should be cited individually. “Basic Information Before Leaving Earth” is a modern interpretive expansion of **BIBLE**, not its linguistic origin.

Historical biblical manuscripts developed across centuries. The Council of Nicaea in 325 CE should not be represented as the event at which Constantine individually selected every book of the modern Bible. The [Codex Sinaiticus Project](https://www.codexsinaiticus.org/en/project/history.aspx) is a source for early manuscript history.
