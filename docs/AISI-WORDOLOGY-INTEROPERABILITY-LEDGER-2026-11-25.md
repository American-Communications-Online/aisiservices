## Purpose

This ledger provides a shared vocabulary for public web infrastructure, semantic interoperability, AI systems, and AISI project terms. It separates:

1. established technical standards;
2. emerging industry terminology;
3. original AISI and Aletheon project vocabulary.

Publication of this ledger does not claim W3C certification, government authority, deployed cross-platform federation, or endorsement by any standards organization.

## Interoperability rules

1. Define every acronym at first use.
2. Record the authoritative source and version.
3. Separate standards from project-created vocabulary.
4. Use stable identifiers and dates.
5. Preserve original wording and provenance.
6. Mark evidence as verified, disputed, unverified, interpretation, testimony, or canon.
7. Record permissions before an agent retrieves, transforms, publishes, or acts.
8. Require human review for consequential decisions and external commitments.
9. Use accessible, semantic formats and stable public links.
10. Treat a planned integration as unverified until its operation is observed.
